JobFinder — Legal

Last updated: November 12, 2026

JobFinder is a local-first, single-user automation tool for LinkedIn: it publishes posts, sends connection invitations and direct-message sequences, and helps you apply to jobs. It runs entirely on your own computer (localhost:3030) and stores everything in local JSON files under ~/.jobfinder/.

Documents

  • Privacy Policy — what data exists, where it lives, and who can see it (answer: only you).
  • Terms of Use — the rules, responsibilities and risks of using JobFinder.

Key facts

  • 100% local storage: posts, invitations, DM cascades, jobs, applications, your resume and every credential are stored only on your machine, in JSON files.
  • No JobFinder servers: the developer operates no backend for this app. No data is ever transmitted to the developer or to any JobFinder server.
  • Cookies stay local: if you connect via session cookies (the "Get cookies.txt LOCALLY" fallback), they are saved in ~/.jobfinder/channels.json and are never shared.
  • Use at your own risk: automation may violate LinkedIn's Terms of Service and can lead to account restrictions. You are responsible for respecting LinkedIn's rate limits and for every action taken with your account.
