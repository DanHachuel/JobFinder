2. What the App is

JobFinder is a local-first, single-user tool that runs on your own computer (localhost:3030) and stores everything in JSON files under ~/.jobfinder/. It automates three LinkedIn workflows: post publishing (manual, scheduled and LLM-generated), outreach (connection invitations and direct-message sequences) and job applications (matching, search and Easy Apply, dry-run by default).
